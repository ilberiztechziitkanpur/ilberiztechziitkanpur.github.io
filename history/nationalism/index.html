✅ What IS in This Year's ICSE Syllabus (100% Covered Here)

  • 1. Factors Leading to Growth of Nationalism: (a) Economic Exploitation (Peasants, Artisans, Working Class, Educated Indians), (b) Repressive Colonial Policies (Lord Lytton's Vernacular Press Act, Arms Act, Delhi Durbar, ICS age reduction & Lord Ripon's Ilbert Bill Controversy), (c) Socio-Religious Reform Movements (specifically contributions of Raja Rammohan Roy and Jyotiba Phule), and (d) Role of the Press.
  • 2. Precursors of the Indian National Congress: Specifically (a) The East India Association (1866 — Dadabhai Naoroji) and (b) The Indian National Association / Indian Association (1876 — Surendranath Banerjee).
  • 3. Foundation & Immediate Objectives of the INC: Foundation by A.O. Hume (Dec 1885), First Two Sessions and their Presidents (1885 Bombay — W.C. Bonnerjee; 1886 Calcutta — Dadabhai Naoroji), and the Four Immediate Objectives of the INC.

🧘‍♂️ 1. Reformers & Precursor Associations Quick-Compare

  • Raja Rammohan Roy: Founded Atmiya Sabha (1815) & Brahmo Sabha / Brahmo Samaj (1828); helped Bentinck pass Abolition of Sati (1829); Journals: Sambad Kaumudi (Bengali) & Mirat-ul-Akhbar (Persian).
  • Jyotiba Phule: Founded Satyashodhak Samaj (1873); wrote Ghulamgiri (Slavery); started First Girls' School at Poona (1848) with wife Savitribai Phule.
  • East India Association (1866): Founded in London by Dadabhai Naoroji to place Indian grievances before the British Parliament and public.
  • Indian Association (1876): Founded in Calcutta by Surendranath Banerjee to unite Indians on a common political programme; convened All-India National Conference (1883).

🏛️ 2. Foundation & First Two Sessions of the INC (1885–1886)

  • Founder & Viceroy (1885): Founded by A.O. Hume (retired British ICS officer); Viceroy was Lord Dufferin.
  • Rename: Originally called Indian National Union (1884); renamed Indian National Congress on the suggestion of Dadabhai Naoroji.
  • 1st Session (Dec 28–31, 1885): Held at Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College, Bombay (shifted from Poona due to cholera/plague); President: W.C. Bonnerjee (72 delegates).
  • 2nd Session (Dec 1886): Held at Calcutta (Kolkata); President: Dadabhai Naoroji (436 delegates).
  • Bonus (3rd & 4th Presidents for 1M MCQs): 3rd Session (1887, Madras) — Badruddin Tyabji (first Muslim President); 4th Session (1888, Allahabad) — George Yule (first English President).
