✅ What IS in This Year's ICSE Syllabus (Included in Vault)

  • 1. Causes of the Quit India Movement: Specifically (a) Failure of the Cripps Mission (1942) and (b) The Japanese Threat (1942).
  • 2. The Quit India Resolution: Wardha Working Committee meeting (July 1942), Bombay All-India Congress Committee ratification (8th August 1942), and Gandhiji's 'Do or Die' call.
  • 3. Significance & Impact of the Movement: All Council Answer Key pointers on depth of nationalism, final blow to British rule, mass participation of all sections, underground second-line leaders (Jai Prakash Narayan, Aruna Asaf Ali, Ram Manohar Lohia), and parallel governments.
