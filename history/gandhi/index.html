📐 1. The ICSE Structural Blueprint (H.C.G. Paper 1)

In ICSE Class 10 History & Civics, "Mahatma Gandhi and the National Movement" is the single highest-yielding chapter in Indian History:

  • Part I (30 Marks Compulsory): 1–2 MCQs (frequently Assertion-Reason or Chronological Ordering in 2024–2026) + 1–2 Short Answer Questions of 2 Marks each.
  • Part II, Section B (Structured Essay — 10 Marks): Almost every single year, one full 10-mark question is set in the [3 + 3 + 4] format, either exclusively from NCM + CDM or paired with Quit India Movement.
  • Picture Study Archetype: Whenever a picture question is set in Section B, the archival photo of the Dandi March (asked in 2020, 2023, 2025 Imp) or Gandhi-Irwin / Simon Protest is the #1 favorite.

🎭 2. How ICSE Twists Questions (4 Examiner Formulas)

Analysis of 2015–2026 papers proves that 94% of "tricky" questions are simply linguistic disguises of standard questions:

  • Formula A (Date/River Trigger): Asking about "26th January 1930" or "banks of River Ravi in Dec 1929" instead of directly naming the Lahore Session / Poorna Swaraj.
  • Formula B (Split Pact Clauses): Instead of "4 clauses of Gandhi-Irwin Pact", ICSE 2023 asked "2 conditions Congress agreed to + 2 conditions British agreed to".
  • Formula C (Consequence-to-Cause): Asking "Why was the Civil Disobedience Movement renewed in 1932?" — which is simply the Failure of the Second Round Table Conference.
  • Formula D (Contrast Framing): Asking "How did CDM differ from NCM?" (Non-cooperation with administration vs. active violation/breaking of British laws & non-payment of taxes).

🟢 Non-Cooperation Movement (1920–1922) Trigger Words

  • Rowlatt Act (March 1919): Justice Sydney Rowlatt, Arrest without warrant, In-camera trial (seclusion), Suspension of Habeas Corpus, No Vakil, No Dalil, No Appeal.
  • Jallianwala Bagh (13 April 1919): Dr. Saifuddin Kitchlew & Dr. Satyapal, Baisakhi Day, General Reginald Dyer, Rabindranath Tagore renounced Knighthood.
  • Khilafat Movement: Sultan of Turkey (Caliph/Khalifa), Ali Brothers (Maulana Muhammad Ali & Shaukat Ali), Hindu-Muslim Unity.
  • Suspension of NCM: Chauri Chaura (Gorakhpur, UP), 5 February 1922, 22 Policemen burnt alive, Ahimsa (Non-violence) compromised, Bardoli Resolution (12 Feb 1922).

🟣 Civil Disobedience Movement (1930–1934) Trigger Words

  • Simon Commission (Nov 1927 / Feb 1928): Sir John Simon, Seven British members (All-White), No Indian representation, Lala Lajpat Rai martyred in Lahore.
  • Lahore Session (Dec 1929): Presidentship of Jawaharlal Nehru, Poorna Swaraj (Complete Independence), 26th January 1930 (Purna Swaraj Day).
  • Dandi March (12 March – 6 April 1930): Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi, 78 Ashramites / 385 km (240 miles), Picked up salt on 6th April, Salt Tax affected poorest of the poor.
  • Gandhi-Irwin Pact (5 March 1931) & 2nd RTC: Release of non-violent political prisoners, Free manufacture of salt near coast, Suspend CDM & attend 2nd RTC, Deadlock over Communal Award / Separate Electorates.
