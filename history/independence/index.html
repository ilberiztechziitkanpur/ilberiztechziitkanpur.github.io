🏛️ 1. Cabinet Mission Plan (March–May 1946)

  • 3 Members: Lord Pethick-Lawrence, Sir Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander.
  • Unity vs Partition: NO Partition! Proposed a Federal Union of British Provinces & Princely States.
  • 3 Union Subjects: Federal Centre controlled only Foreign Affairs, Defence, and Communications (Residuary powers with Provinces).
  • 3 Groups of Provinces: Group A (6 Hindu-majority: Madras, Bombay, UP, CP, Bihar, Orissa), Group B (Punjab, NWFP, Sindh), Group C (Bengal & Assam).
  • Constituent Assembly: 389 Members (296 British Provinces + 93 Princely States) + Interim Government of 14 members.

📜 2. Mountbatten Plan (June 3, 1947)

  • Author: Lord Louis Mountbatten (Last Viceroy of British India).
  • Partition Proposal: Partition of India into two Dominions—India and Pakistan.
  • Bengal & Punjab: Legislative Assemblies to meet in two parts to decide on partition; Boundary Commission (Sir Cyril Radcliffe) to mark borders.
  • Referendums: NWFP (plebiscite/referendum) and Sylhet district of Assam (referendum) to decide whether to join India or Pakistan; Sindh Assembly to vote directly.
  • Princely States: Treaties with Crown end; free to join India or Pakistan or remain independent.

⚖️ 3. Indian Independence Act (July 18 / Aug 15, 1947)

  • Legal Effect: Passed by British Parliament; created Two Independent Dominions (India & Pakistan) from August 15, 1947.
  • Executive Head: Separate Governor-General for each Dominion appointed by British King on advice of Dominion Cabinet.
  • Legislature: Existing Constituent Assemblies to serve as Central Legislatures with full sovereign power.
  • End of British Rule: Office of Secretary of State for India abolished; title 'Emperor of India' dropped; British parliamentary jurisdiction ended.
  • Interim Law & Assets: Governed by Government of India Act 1935 until new Constitution framed; Division of Indian Army and Assets.
