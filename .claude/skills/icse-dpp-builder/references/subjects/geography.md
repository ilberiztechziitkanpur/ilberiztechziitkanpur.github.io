Verification: pattern VERIFIED from ICSE 2027 Specimen (QP + draft MS); syllabus VERIFIED (2027 PDF). Specimen toposheet = Survey of India Map Sheet G43S10 (the board sheet changes every year). 2026 board paper not cross-checked (unverified).

## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 2 hours (+15 min reading). 80 marks + 20 internal assessment. Part I (30, compulsory) + Part II (50, attempt any 5 of 9 questions Q4-Q12, 10 marks each).
- To be supplied: SOI map sheet extract (must be returned) + outline map of India (detached, marked, fastened to answer booklet). "Make wise use of arrows"; sub-parts must be answered in serial order.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 10 | Topographical map (SOI sheet) - 5 sub-questions each (a)(b) 1 mark: Q1(i) meaning of contour/spot height figure & RF; (ii) transport / actual length by scale (distance stones); (iii) settlement evidence + area in km^2 by grid squares; (iv) conventional symbol by six-figure reference + drainage pattern; (v) causeway use, significance of fire lines. Types: Recall/Understanding/Application/Analysis; includes calculation (area/distance), grid references, 4/6-figure references. |
| Q2 | 10 | Outline map of India: (i) mark/name 6 items (a-f) e.g. hill range where Eastern & Western Ghats meet (Nilgiris), Wular Lake, SP over sparsely populated area, shade black soil, Gulf of Kutch, label wind bringing rain to Mumbai; (ii) identify A (river = Kaveri), B river, C coalfield, D riverine port (4 items) |
| Q3 | 10 | 10 MCQs x 1: factual (state-wise crops/minerals/steel plants), analytical, analogy (Black soil : Maharashtra :: Red soil : __), assertion-reason (iv), incorrectly matched pair, transport mode choice, plastic degradation |
| Q4 | 10 | Climate: (i) 2 (a)(b) Application/Analysis; (ii) 2 monsoon branches; (iii) 3 reasons; (iv) 3 climatic data table (max rainfall month, annual range of temperature, reason for July drop) |
| Q5 | 10 | Soil: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (conservation, similarities of red & laterite, black soil (a)(b)(c), reason-for statements) |
| Q6 | 10 | Natural Vegetation: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (tropical evergreen forest, Van Mahotsav, desert vegetation, forests "Green Gold") |
| Q7 | 10 | Water Resources: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (drip irrigation, canal irrigation, tube wells, picture-based) |
| Q8 | 10 | Minerals & Energy: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (biogas plants, picture-based, reasons, image-based) |
| Q9 | 10 | Agriculture: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (agriculture/Green Revolution/cash crops/picture-based) |
| Q10 | 10 | Manufacturing industries: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (steel plant location, SSI, reasons, Mumbai locational advantages) |
| Q11 | 10 | Transport: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (road vs rail, perishables, railway problems, reasons) |
| Q12 | 10 | Waste Management: 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 (picture-identify disposal method, 3R, recycling, household measures) |

- Part II question order follows syllabus unit order (Climate -> Waste Management). Each Q = (i) 2 + (ii) 2 + (iii) 3 + (iv) 3. Picture/image-based sub-parts appear in Q7-Q9, Q12 (and Q8). Internal choice: any 5 of 9.
- Compulsory: toposheet Q1, map-of-India Q2, MCQ Q3. Part II answers: 1 mark per point.

## 3. Marking conventions
- Part II: 1 mark per valid point/reason; "state two" = 2 points; "give reasons" = 1 mark each reason for a, b, c; 3-mark parts list/explain 3 points.
- Map work: name must be written; correct location, arrows, neat labels; incorrect/over-crowded labels lose marks; shade vs mark vs write abbreviation as instructed (e.g. "SP"). Identification (ii) = name only.
- Toposheet: answers exact (grid ref 4-fig/6-fig, km^2 area = grid squares counted, actual length = map length x scale). Write units. Use legend conventional signs.
- Climatic data: show calculation (annual range = max - min temperature).
- Time: 1.5 min per mark; Q1 ~15 min, Q2 ~15 min.

## 5. Syllabus chapters (Class X, 2027)
Part I - Map Work: 1 Interpretation of Topographical Maps (SOI 1:50,000 sheet: grid references, contours, drainage, settlements, vegetation, transport, scale, direction, landforms); 2 Map of India (physical features: mountains, rivers, lakes, ports, plateaus, soil/vegetation/mineral distribution, cities).
Part II - Geography of India: 3 Climate; 4 Soil Resources; 5 Natural Vegetation; 6 Water Resources; 7 Mineral & Energy Resources; 8 Agriculture; 9 Manufacturing Industries; 10 Transport; 11 Waste Management.
- Chapters recently changed: UNVERIFIED. Known history: "Waste Management" and "Natural Vegetation/Water Resources" wording changed after 2018; "Population", "Location & extent / Physical features of India" chapters were dropped from the board theory paper in the current format (check older PYQs); older "Forests / Wildlife / Human settlements" topics are out of scope.

## 6. Gotchas
- Q1 and Q2 need the actual SOI sheet and outline map: either embed images (SVG/PNG) with clearly stated grid references, or exclude. Do not invent toposheet data.
- Map of India answers vary by exact map; use place name + approximate location text for MCQ-style variants.
- Facts change: leading states (jute W.Bengal, cotton Gujarat/Maharashtra), steel plants public/private; verify with textbook/latest data.
- Assertion-reason uses the 4-option set. Picture-based parts need a text description of the picture.
- Answers in "keyword" style, not long essays; 3-mark = 3 points.
