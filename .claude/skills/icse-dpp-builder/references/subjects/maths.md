# ICSE Class 10 - Mathematics (code 511)

Verification: pattern VERIFIED from CISCE ICSE 2027 Specimen (QP + draft MS, text read); syllabus VERIFIED from 2027 syllabus PDF. Board-2026 cross-check: only secondary (Section A = 40 marks confirmed by a news snippet); unverified in detail.
Research date 22 Nov 2026. CISCE states: "format of the Board paper will remain the same as the Specimen". ~50% of marks are competency (higher-order) tagged.

## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 3 hours (+15 min reading, no writing). Max marks 80 + 20 internal assessment. Mathematical tables allowed. "Omission of essential working will result in loss of marks."
- Section A (40, all compulsory), Section B (40, attempt any 4 of 7 = Q4-Q10, 10 marks each).

| Q | Marks | Content (2027 specimen) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 15 | 15 objective items, 1 mark each, "write only the correct option": plain MCQ, assertion-reason (Q1 ii, xiii), diagram based (tangents Q1 xi), word/case problems (GST, RD, shares, G.P., map scale, probability, matrices) |
| Q2 | 12 | (i) 4 mensuration, 3-part (a)(b)(c) with "nearest whole number" and pi=22/7; (ii) 4 quadratic equation to 3 s.f.; (iii) 4 coordinate geometry (section formula, ratio, equation of perpendicular line) |
| Q3 | 13 | (i) 4 GST word problem (inverse/discount); (ii) 4 A.P. (a)(b)(c); (iii) 5 graph-reading reflection question (coordinates, reflection in y-axis/origin, image of polygon, equation of line) |
| Q4 | 10 | (i) 3 RD (a)(b); (ii) 4 matrices (AB null matrix, find x); (iii) 3 trig identity proof |
| Q5 | 10 | (i) 3 shares/dividend; (ii) 3 linear inequation + number line; (iii) 4 circles (angle properties, figure) |
| Q6 | 10 | (i) 3 remainder/factor theorem (factorise); (ii) 3 similarity/BPT, ratio of areas (a)(b)(c); (iii) 4 ruler-compass construction (circle/tangent/loci) |
| Q7 | 10 | (i) 3 probability (cards 1-50); (ii) 3 tangents/circumscribed parallelogram proof (a)(b); (iii) 4 ratio-proportion (componendo-dividendo / proof) |
| Q8 | 10 | (i) 3 mean of distribution; (ii) 3 shaded-area mensuration forming equation; (iii) 4 cylinder/solids word problem |
| Q9 | 10 | (i) 5 grouped data: median/ogive-based (a)(b)(c) (draw ogive); (ii) 5 trig heights & distances (draw diagram) |
| Q10 | 10 | (i) 3 G.P.; (ii) 3 tangents (a)(b); (iii) 4 histogram + modal weight (scale given) |

- Split pattern: Section B questions are 3+3+4 except Q9 = 5+5. Q4-Q10 mark layout above taken from bracket values in the specimen; topic assignment per (i)/(ii)/(iii) is as printed in 2027 specimen but a board paper may reshuffle topics.
- Internal choice: only "any 4 of 7" in Section B; none inside Section A.
- Objective count: 15 in Q1 (incl. >=2 assertion-reason). Case/word-based sub-parts also appear in Q2/Q3. Graph/diagram-based: Q3(iii) graph, Q9 ogive/diagram, Q10 histogram, construction Q6(iii).
- Compulsory drawing items: construction (3-4 marks), graph/histogram/ogive (5/4 marks), heights-and-distances diagram.

## 3. Marking conventions
- Method marks: 3-mark = 3 steps (formula/substitution, working, answer); 4-mark = 4 steps; final-answer-only earns little; follow-through marks given for consequential errors.
- Units: area cm^2, volume cm^3, money Rs; omit units -> usually 1 mark loss in mensuration/commercial maths.
- Approximation instructions (nearest whole number / 3 s.f. / 2 decimals / pi=22/7) are marked.
- Diagrams: figure for heights/distances is part of marks; constructions need arcs left visible, labelled; graph/histogram/ogive marks for scale, plotting, smooth curve, reading.
- Proofs: statement-reason pairs; trig identity proofs need LHS->RHS in steps.
- Time: ~1.5 min per mark (3 h for 80 marks incl. revision); Section A 15 MCQs ~25 min.

## 5. Syllabus chapters (Class X, 2027 syllabus)
1 Commercial Mathematics: GST (incl. inverse), Banking (Recurring Deposit), Shares & Dividends. 2 Algebra: Linear Inequations; Quadratic Equations (nature of roots, solve, word problems); Ratio & Proportion (continued/mean proportion, componendo-dividendo); Factorisation (Factor & Remainder Theorem, deg<=3); Matrices (2x2); A.P. & G.P.; Coordinate Geometry (reflection, section & midpoint formula incl. centroid, equation of a line, parallel/perpendicular). 3 Geometry: Similarity (BPT, areas, maps/models); Loci (proofs not required); Circles (angle, cyclic, tangent & secant properties; constructions of tangents, circumcircle/incircle of triangle and hexagon). 4 Mensuration: cylinder, cone, sphere, combined solids (frustum excluded). 5 Trigonometry: identities; heights & distances (2-D, tables). 6 Statistics: mean, median, mode, ogive, histogram. 7 Probability (simple).

Older papers (<2020) may contain Banking/Shares beyond this scope, Loci with proofs, "Sales Tax/VAT" (replaced by GST from 2018), Trigonometry of complementary angles/ values of standard angles, Co-ordinate geometry distance formula/ Compound Interest, Similarity/Constructions variants - treat pre-2019 PYQs as possibly out of syllabus (UNVERIFIED which exact chapters).

## 6. Gotchas for building practice questions
- Provide step-wise worked solutions (method marks); numeric answers must be re-computed, never copied from a third-party key unchecked.
- Many questions depend on figures (circles, similar triangles, heights-distances, graphs, histogram, ogive): need SVG or described figure; Q3(iii) and Q9/Q10 are graph-based - generate datasets and plots, or exclude.
- Constructions and graphs cannot be auto-checked in an MCQ DPP: convert to "identify result / measure / reading" questions or provide a model figure.
- Use Rs and cm/m consistently; specify rounding.
- GST problems: state intra-state (CGST+SGST half each) vs inter-state IGST; 2026-27 specimens use GST only.
- Assertion-reason option order is fixed: (a) A true R false (b) A false R true (c) both true, R explains A (d) both true, R does not explain A - keep exactly (2027 specimen order).
- Pi = 22/7 only if stated; some answers "correct to 3 s.f.".
- Mathematical tables (log/trig) permitted - trig heights questions may need tables.
