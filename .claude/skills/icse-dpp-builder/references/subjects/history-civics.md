## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 2 hours (+15 min reading, no writing). 80 marks theory + 20 internal assessment. Part I compulsory (30); Part II (50) = Section A Civics (any 2 of 3) 20 + Section B History (any 3 of 5) 30.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 16 | 16 objective x 1: MCQ, assertion-reason, chronology/sequence, match, picture/cartoon/source/diagram-identify (Civics + History) |
| Q2 (i)-(vii) | 14 | 7 short answers x 2 marks: extract/picture/case based, "any two" points |
| Q3 | 10 | Civics - Union Legislature: (i) 3 + (ii) 3 + (iii) 4 |
| Q4 | 10 | Civics - Executive (President/PM/Council of Ministers): 3 + 3 + 4 |
| Q5 | 10 | Civics - Judiciary (Supreme/High Court): 3 + 4 + 3 |
| Q6 | 10 | History - 1857 revolt / British rule (source based): 3 + 3 + 4 |
| Q7 | 10 | History - Early Nationalists / Press / Congress: 3 + 3 + 4 |
| Q8 | 10 | History - Mass phase (Dandi March, Quit India...): 3 + 3 + 4 |
| Q9 | 10 | History - World Wars / dictatorships: 3 + 3 + 4 |
| Q10 | 10 | History - United Nations (ICJ, GA...) + heritage: 3 + 3 + 4 |

- Internal choice: Civics any 2 of 3; History any 3 of 5. Objective count = 16 MCQ-type. Picture/source/cartoon items appear in Q1, Q2 and Section B.
- The topic assignment per question (Q6 = 1857 etc.) is taken from the verified pattern.js; the board may swap chapters between questions (e.g. Q7/Q8 History). Muslim League/Lucknow Pact are a separate chapter in the repo.
- Older formats: 2016-2020 Part I short-answer (no MCQ), Part II (a)3+(b)3+(c)4; 2021 no exam; 2022 Semester 1 (MCQ) / Semester 2 (subjective); 2023-24 current format; verify details in pattern.js.

## 3. Marking conventions
- 1 mark objective: write only option letter; 2-mark = 2 distinct points (1 each, "any two"); 3-mark = 3 points; 4-mark = 4 explained points (or 2+2), ~60-80 words; keywords/names/dates/Acts rewarded; repeated points count once; no negative marking; do not write more than asked (extra points earn nothing).
- Time ~1.5 min per mark.

## 5. Syllabus chapters (Class X, 2027)
Section A Civics: 1 The Union Legislature (Parliament: composition, powers, Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha, Speaker, bills, anti-defection); 2 The Union Executive (President, Vice-President, PM & Council of Ministers); 3 The Judiciary (Supreme Court, High Court, subordinate courts, Lok Adalat).
Section B History: 1 The Indian National Movement (1857-1917): 1857 Revolt, reform movements, foundation of INC, First Phase (Early Nationalists), Second Phase (Radicals, Partition of Bengal, Surat Split), Muslim League & Lucknow Pact; 2 Mass Phase of the National Movement (1915-1947): Gandhi, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience (Dandi), Quit India, INA, Independence & Partition; 3 The Contemporary World: Rise of Dictatorships (Fascism, Nazism), First World War, Second World War, the United Nations (GA, SC, ICJ, agencies), Cold War/NAM (check exact scope in PDF).
- Deleted/added chapters vs older syllabi: UNVERIFIED. Treat pre-2018 PYQs on topics outside the list above (e.g. Class IX-type constitution/election topics) as out of syllabus; check wording in the 2027 PDF.

## 6. Gotchas
- Source/picture based items need the source text or image description; do not invent.
- Date/name facts must be exact (INC 1885 A.O. Hume, W.C. Bonnerjee; Partition of Bengal 1905; Surat 1907; Dandi 1930).
- Keep 'any two' phrasing: answers with exactly the requested number of points.
- Chronology/AR options fixed set.
