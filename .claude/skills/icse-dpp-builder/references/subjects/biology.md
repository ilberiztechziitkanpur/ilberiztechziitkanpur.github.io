## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 2 hours (+15 min reading). 80 marks + 20 internal (practical). Section A compulsory (40); Section B attempt any 4 of 6 (Q3-Q8), 10 marks each.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 15 | 15 MCQ x 1: scenario/case MCQs (Q1 i smell of cut grass, ii dizzy cricketer, vii cucumber salad/osmosis, ix salivation, xiii tongue rolling), assertion-reason (iii, xii), data/graph (iv urea levels), statement-correctness (vi), flow sequence (x pulmonary circulation), food chain (xi), ecology (xiv), root hair (xv) |
| Q2 | 25 | five parts x 5 marks: (i) 5 give the biological/technical term (5 one-word); (ii) 5 fill in the blanks in a passage (ovaries, 5 x 1; answers ova, ovulation, fertilisation, uterus, 40); (iii) 5 choose the ODD term out (O) and name the common category (C) (5 x 1... marking [5x1]); (iv) 5 case (earache - Eustachian tube, ossicles, organ of Corti, functions); (v) 5 study labelled diagram and match structure with function (placenta, umbilical cord ...) |
| Q3 | 10 | case (wilted plant, transpiration) sub-parts 1+2+2+2+3: name process (1), define (2), rainy day (2), stomata + location (2), three adaptations (3) |
| Q4 | 10 | (i) case (hormonal disorder - hirsutism): (a) disorder name 1, (b) hormone + gland 2, (c) define hormone 2, (d) expand TSH, ACTH 2 = 7; (ii) 3 marks draw neat labelled diagram of turgid root hair cell |
| Q5 | 10 | picture-based (pollution/population): message of picture (1), two types of pollution (2), two reasons for population explosion (2), two health problems (2), three resources under pressure (3) |
| Q6 | 10 | cell division & genetics: type of cell division for wound healing (2), chromosomes in human cell (2), Mendel's Law of Segregation (2), why haemophilia uncommon in females (2), draw labelled diagram of animal cell with 4 chromosomes (3 - total may include 1) |
| Q7 | 10 | experiment-based (starch test/photosynthesis): aim (1), balanced equation + mineral element (2), conclusion after iodine test (2), hormone for phototropism + tropic movement (2), draw labelled stomatal apparatus (3) |
| Q8 | 10 | mixed: natural selection proposer (1), Australopithecus features (2), hyperopia cause + correction (2), identical vs fraternal twin difference (2), draw labelled human blood smear (3) |

- Pattern: every Section B question = 10 marks, 4-5 sub-parts, typically ending with a 3-mark labelled diagram (Q4-Q8) or a 3-mark "list three". Internal choice: any 4 of 6; no inner choice.
- Competency tags per part: Recall, Understanding, Application, Analysis (about 50% higher-order).
- Diagram questions: Q4(ii), Q6(v), Q7(v), Q8(v) (+ labelled-picture match in Q2(v)). Compulsory diagram marks are in Section B (each 3).

## 3. Marking conventions
- 1 mark per correct point/keyword; "state two ..." = 2 distinct points; "differentiate" = 2 pairs of contrasting points (1 each) or a labelled table.
- Diagrams (3 marks): neat outline 1 (proportion), correct labelling 2 (any 4-6 labels as asked); pencil, label lines straight, do not cross. Title/name on diagram.
- Definitions: precise keyword; functions: one-liners; balanced equation for photosynthesis counts as 1-2 marks (with light + chlorophyll over arrow).
- Time: ~1.5 min per mark; 3-mark diagram ~4 min.

## 5. Syllabus chapters (Class X, 2027)
1 Basic Biology: Cell Cycle & Cell Division (mitosis, meiosis); Structure of chromosome; Genetics (Mendel's laws, monohybrid, sex determination, sex-linked inheritance). 2 Plant Physiology: absorption by roots (imbibition, diffusion, osmosis, active transport), transpiration, photosynthesis (+ experiments), chemical coordination in plants (auxins etc.), tropisms. 3 Human Anatomy & Physiology: Circulatory (blood, lymph, heart), Excretory (kidney, nephron, dialysis), Nervous system & sense organs (eye, ear), Endocrine system, Reproductive system (menstrual cycle, hormones). 4 Population (growth, explosion, control). 5 Human Evolution (Darwin, Australopithecus -> Homo sapiens, natural selection). 6 Pollution (air, water, soil, noise, radioactive, waste management).
- Deleted/added vs older syllabi: UNVERIFIED. Old papers (pre-2018) contained 'Health & Hygiene', 'Economic importance/ Conservation', 'Pollution control', 'Biotechnology'-type questions and "Respiration/Nutrition" in different form; check each old PYQ vs the 2027 PDF.

## 6. Gotchas
- Diagrams are heavily tested; build SVG/labelled-text answers, or MCQ "identify label" format.
- Case-based sets need a short scenario then sub-parts - copy the 1+2+2+2+3 mark structure.
- Terms must be exact (e.g. "lenticels", "aqueous humour", "inguinal canal", "meiosis", "acetylcholine" for Q2(i)-type).
- Hormone/gland/disorder pairings must be textbook-correct (check against the specimen marking scheme for Q4(i)).
- Keep spellings correct; examiners reward keywords.
