## 1. Exam pattern
- One paper, 3 hours (syllabus: "three hours"; unverified against 2027 timetable), 80 marks + 20 internal assessment (language + literature assignments, oral). Section A (40, compulsory, language) + Section B (40, prescribed texts).
- Section B: "Questions from only two textbooks are to be answered. Attempt four questions; at least one from each of the two books you studied and any two others from the same books."

## 3. Marking conventions
- Composition (15): content/ideas and language (vishay-vastu + bhasha/shaili); ~250 words. Letter (7): format (pata, dinank, sambodhan, abhivadan, vishay, samapti) + content; ~120 words.
- Comprehension: answers in own words (2 marks = 2 points/one complete sentence).
- Grammar: 1 mark per correct item; spelling/matra errors lose marks.
- Extracts: 2-mark = short complete answers; 3-mark = 3 points / explain with reference to context (prasang-sandarbh); answers in Hindi.
- Time: ~2.2 min per mark (3 h for 80 marks); composition 30 min.

## 5. Syllabus (Class X, 2027)
Section A: composition, letter, unseen comprehension, grammar (vocabulary, syntax, idioms, synthesis). Section B (prescribed - Sahitya Sagar, Evergreen): Short stories - Bade Ghar Ki Beti (Premchand), Sandeh (Jaishankar Prasad), Bheed Mein Khoya Aadmi (Leeladhar Sharma Parvatiya), Bhedein Aur Bhediye (Harishankar Parsai), Do Kalakaar (Mannu Bhandari). Poems - Sur Ke Pad (Surdas), Vinay Ke Pad (Tulsidas), Bhikshuk (Nirala), Chalna Hamara Kaam Hai (Shivmangal Singh 'Suman'), Matri Mandir Ki Or (Subhadra Kumari Chauhan). Novel: Naya Raasta - Sushma Agarwal, Sarg 14 to 26. Ekanki Sanchay (ICSE One-Act Plays): Sookhi Daali (Upendranath Ashk), Mahabharat Ki Ek Saanjh (Bharat Bhushan Agrawal), Deepdaan (Ram Kumar Verma) (list shown in Appendix I for 2027 may have more titles; verify in the PDF).
