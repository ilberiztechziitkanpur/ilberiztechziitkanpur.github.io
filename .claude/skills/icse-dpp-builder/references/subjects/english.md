# ICSE Class 10 - English Language (Paper 1, code 011) and Literature in English (Paper 2, code 012)

Verification: both patterns VERIFIED from ICSE 2027 Specimen (QP + draft MS); syllabus + prescribed texts VERIFIED (2027 syllabus + Appendix I). 2026 board paper not cross-checked (unverified).
Research date 22 Nov 2026. Two separate 80-mark papers (+20 internal each: Paper 1 = listening + speaking; Paper 2 = literature assignments).

## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)

### Paper 1 - English Language (2 hours, 80 marks; attempt all 5 questions)
Advice printed: spend <=30 min on Q1 and <=20 min on Q2.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 20 | Composition 300-350 words, choose 1 of 5 topics: (i) story starting with a given sentence, (ii) narrative/account (punished for something not done), (iii) argumentative (for/against proverb), (iv) descriptive/reflective (class project), (v) picture-based composition. Competency: Creativity/Evaluation |
| Q2 | 10 | Letter, choose 1 of 2 (informal to grandfather / formal to Director of Museum) |
| Q3 | 10 | (i) 5 Notice (poster-designing competition); (ii) 5 Email (invite artist to judge) - both compulsory |
| Q4 | 20 | Unseen reading passage (~450-500 words, narrative, "A Day's Wait" adaptation): (i) 2 word meanings (2 x 1 MCQ); (ii) 1 antonym MCQ; (iii) short answers (a)2 (b)2 (c)2 (d)1 (e)2 = 9; (iv) 8 summary/précis in <=50 words (content ~6 points + expression 2) |
| Q5 | 20 | Grammar: (i) 4 fill in correct form of verb (8 blanks; 0.5 each inferred from [4] total); (ii) 4 prepositions (8 single-word blanks; 0.5 each inferred); (iii) 4 sentence synthesis - 4 MCQ items "without and/but/so" (1 each); (iv) 8 transformation/rewriting, now MCQ-format with options (indirect speech, passive, comparison degree, concession "In spite of", conditional "If", "No sooner", "so...that", question tag) 8 items x 1 |

- No internal choice except composition (1 of 5) and letter (1 of 2). In 2027 specimen the grammar items (iii) and (iv) are objective (choose correct option) - a change from older fully written transformations (older papers: written "rewrite as directed" items - format UNVERIFIED). Count: ~20 objective items (reading 3 MCQ-style, grammar 16 MCQ-style).
- Competency tagging: Understanding, Application, Recall, Creativity.

### Paper 2 - Literature in English (2 hours, 80 marks)
Four sections. Section A compulsory; attempt one question from each of B, C, D and one other question from any section of your choice (4 x 16 = 64 + 16 = 80).

| Section | Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Q1 | 16 | 16 MCQ x 1 across all texts (drama 6: Julius Caesar; poems 5; short stories 5), incl. figure-of-speech, sequence of events, statement I/II relation, inference |
| B Drama | Q2, Q3 | 16 each | Extract-based on Julius Caesar (Acts III-V): (i) 3 + (ii) 3 + (iii) 3 + (iv) 3 + (v) 4 (explain lines) |
| C Prose (Short stories) | Q4, Q5 | 16 each | Extract/passage based: The Elevator (Q4), The Last Lesson (Q5) in specimen; 3+3+3+3+4 |
| D Poetry | Q6, Q7 | 16 each | Poem extract based: The Glove and the Lions (Q6), A Considerable Speck (Q7); 3+3+3+3+4 |

- "Answer one or more questions" from each section (internal choice = 2 questions per section; pick 1 + 1 extra). Every extract question = 5 sub-parts (4 x 3 marks + one 4-mark "explain/describe/in your own words").

## 2. DPP section mapping (suggested ids)
Paper 1: `p1-composition` (Q1 20) - prompts + model essay; `p1-letter` (Q2, formal & informal); `p1-notice-email` (Q3, notice + email); `p1-comprehension` (Q4 incl. summary); `p1-grammar-verb` (Q5(i)); `p1-grammar-prep` (Q5(ii)); `p1-grammar-synthesis` (Q5(iii)); `p1-grammar-transform` (Q5(iv)).
Paper 2: `p2-mcq` (Q1 16 MCQ); `p2-drama` (Julius Caesar extracts, 16 marks each); `p2-prose` (short story extracts); `p2-poetry` (poem extracts).
Optional topic DPPs: tenses, concord, reported speech, voice, conditionals, question tags, phrasal verbs, idioms.

## 3. Marking conventions
- Composition (20): content and expression marks (roughly 10 + 10); word count 300-350; relevance, organisation, vocabulary, grammar. Letter (10): format (address, date, salutation, subscription) + content; informal/formal. Notice (5): heading, date, event details, signature; word limit ~50 (UNVERIFIED). Email (5): subject, salutation, body, close; word limit UNVERIFIED (check board instructions).
- Comprehension: "in your own words" answers; 1 mark per point; summary in <=50 words: content points + expression (language) marks.
- Grammar: each correct blank/option = 0.5-1 mark; answers must be exact (spelling counts).
- Literature extracts: explain with reference to context; 3-mark = 3 points; 4-mark "explain lines" = paraphrase + significance; answers in own words from text only.
- Time: 1.5 min per mark; Q1 composition 30 min.

## 5. Syllabus (Class X, 2027 - prescribed texts)
- Paper 1 Language: composition, letter/notice/email, comprehension (unseen, summary), grammar (tenses, voice, speech, prepositions, synthesis, transformations); no prescribed book.
- Paper 2 Literature (Class X): Drama - Julius Caesar (Acts III, IV, V); Prose (Treasure Chest, Evergreen) - With the Photographer (Leacock), The Elevator (Sleator), The Girl Who Can (Aidoo), The Pedestrian (Bradbury), The Last Lesson (Daudet); Poetry - Haunted Houses (Longfellow), The Glove and the Lions (Leigh Hunt), When Great Trees Fall (Maya Angelou), A Considerable Speck (Frost), The Power of Music (Sukumar Ray).
- Class IX content (Acts I-II, Bonku Babu's Friend ..., The Night Mail etc.) is NOT examined in Class X. Deleted/added vs older years: UNVERIFIED; the current anthology "Treasure Chest" replaced older "Treasure Trove"/"Merchant of Venice" era texts - older PYQs on those texts are out of syllabus (only Julius Caesar extracts/ questions on common acts may still be usable after checking Act III-V).

## 6. Gotchas
- Grammar answers must be exact sentences/words; supply answer key with the single correct option; for written transformations provide official-style model sentence.
- Do not reproduce long copyrighted extracts beyond short quotations; extract-based Qs need short extract text (provide line refs).
- Letter/notice/email formats strictly per CISCE; give model answers.
- Composition DPPs cannot be auto-graded: provide outline, marking rubric (content/expression) and a model answer.
- Literature MCQs and extract answers must be text-faithful (character, act/scene refs).
