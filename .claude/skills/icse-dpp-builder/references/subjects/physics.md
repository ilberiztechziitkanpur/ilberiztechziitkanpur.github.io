## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 2 hours (+15 min reading, no writing). 80 marks theory + 20 internal (practical). Section A compulsory (40); Section B attempt any 4 of 6 (Q4-Q9), 10 marks each = 40.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 15 | 15 MCQ x 1: plain MCQ, diagram/figure based (lever/seesaw Q1 i, light in trough Q1 vi, magnet+coil Q1 xiii, circuit Q1 xv), assertion-reason (viii), statement/sequence, relationship items; conceptual and numerical-reasoning (e.g. "height of A is one-sixth of B") |
| Q2 | 10 | (i) 6 x 1 fill-ups "choose from bracket" (some 2-blank pairs, answers marked [2]); (ii) 2 match the column (body-part levers vs lever class); (iii) 2 graph analysis (displacement-position graph) |
| Q3 | 15 | (i)-(vi) 2 marks each = 12 (short answer/diagram/data based: lens made of two materials, kWh meter readings, centre of gravity of pentagon, true/false with reason, wire & magnet, masses m..5m); (vii) 3 marks application (fibre optics, TIR) |
| Q4-Q9 | 10 each | 3 + 3 + 4. Q4 refraction/prism (ray diagram, deviation graph, lens-screen distance numerical); Q5 concave lens ray diagram, cooling curve, calorimetry numerical; Q6 moment/levers, work by gravity, trolley kinetic energy/work-energy numerical; Q7 lever/machine numerical, echo/sound (speed, two walls), resonance/wires; Q8 current electricity (parallel circuit, fuse rating 2kW 220V, resistivity ratio); Q9 electricity/EMI/ radioactivity (mass number, isotopes, alpha-beta, electric field shift) |

- Internal choice: any 4 of 6 in Section B. No choice inside parts.
- Numericals typically 3-4 marks each, 1 mark per step. Diagram-drawing questions (ray diagram, circuit, graph) 2-3 marks. Competency-tagged (Application/Analysis/Evaluation) ~50%.

## 3. Marking conventions
- Numericals: write formula (1), substitution (1), answer with unit (1/2); unit omission costs marks; follow-through marks given. Show given data.
- Diagrams: ray diagram needs correct rays + arrows + labels (marks per ray, 3 marks = e.g. 1+1+1); circuit symbols standard; graphs - labelled axes with units.
- Theory: 1 mark per distinct point; "state two reasons" = 2 distinct points; definitions with keyword (e.g. "per unit mass", "rise in temperature of 1 K").
- Time: 1.5 min per mark; numerical 4-marks ~6 min.

## 6. Gotchas
- Include units and significant figures; use g=10 m/s^2 only if stated (check stem).
- Ray diagrams/circuits: need SVG; MCQs on figures need described or drawn diagram - flag `picture` text.
- Numericals: verify answer by calculation; many board numericals use "specific heat capacity of water 4200 J/kg K", "latent heat of ice 336000 J/kg", "speed of sound ~ 330/340 m/s" - state in stem.
- Statements for assertion-reason use the 4-option fixed set.
- Fill-ups "choose from bracket" may require two blanks per sub-part.
