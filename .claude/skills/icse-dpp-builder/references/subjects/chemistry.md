## 1. Exam pattern (2027 specimen)
- 2 hours (+15 min reading). 80 marks + 20 internal (practical). Section A compulsory (40); Section B attempt any 4 of 6 (Q3-Q8), 10 marks each.

| Q | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 15 | 15 MCQ x 1: plain MCQ, diagram-based (industrial process Q1 ii, metal + dil. HCl Q1 v, reaction demonstration Q1 xiv), statement I/II (vii), assertion-reason (viii), data/test-tube (ix), organic (x, xi), lone-pair/mole (xii, xv) |
| Q2 | 25 | five parts x 5 marks: (i) 5 diagram-based (electrolysis of acidulated water, 5 sub-questions); (ii) 5 write balanced chemical equations (5 x 1); (iii) 5 complete sentences by choosing the correct option from a bracket; (iv) 5 match Column A with Column B; (v) 5 = (a) IUPAC names + (b) draw structural diagrams of organic compounds |
| Q3 | 10 | 2+2+3+3: (i) formula/compound ID (2); (ii) give observation (2); (iii) salt analysis - blue crystalline salt B (3, (a)(b)(c)); (iv) metal N / basic oxide (3) |
| Q4 | 10 | 3+3+4: (i) Name the following (3); (ii) pH 2/7/13 solutions (3); (iii) iron chloride R + NaOH (4, identify R, precipitate etc.) |
| Q5 | 10 | 2+2+3+3: (i) mole concept (2); (ii) state with reasons (2); (iii) balanced equations for conversions (3); (iv) periodic table position of elements P, Q (3) |
| Q6 | 10 | 3+3+4: (i) identify reactants P,Q,R (3); (ii) ammonium dichromate decomposition (3); (iii) NaCl vs NH4Cl test-tube diagram experiment (4) |
| Q7 | 10 | 2+2+3+3: (i) ratio of methane to acidic gas product (2); (ii) atoms P,Q structure diagram (2); (iii) balanced equations (3); (iv) esterification flask (ethanol+acetic acid+conc. H2SO4) (3) |
| Q8 | 10 | 3+3+4: (i) reactivity series metal Z (3); (ii) electron-dot structures (3); (iii) five organic structures C-G (4) |

- Internal choice: any 4 of 6. Objective count in Section A: 15 MCQ + 15 objective-style (fill/match/equations) items in Q2. Competency tags: Understanding/Application/Analysis.
- Compulsory in Section A: balanced equations (Q2 ii), match (Q2 iv), IUPAC+structures (Q2 v). Diagram/practical-skill based questions in Q2(i), Q6(iii).

## 3. Marking conventions
- Equations: balanced + conditions/catalyst/state symbols as taught; unbalanced = 0 for that equation (1 mark each). Observations: colour/gas/precipitate and its colour (and "soluble in excess" where relevant).
- Names: IUPAC (e.g. propan-1-ol, ethanoic acid); structural formulae show all bonds. Electron-dot structures show lone pairs/shared pairs.
- Numericals: formula/relation, substitution, answer with unit (mole, g, dm^3/litres at STP); atomic masses given in paper.
- Reasons: 1 mark per valid scientific reason; one-word answers for "name".
- Time: ~1.5 min per mark; Q2 (25 marks) ~35 min.

## 5. Syllabus chapters (Class X, 2027)
1 Periodic Properties & Variations of Properties; 2 Chemical Bonding (electrovalent, covalent, coordinate); 3 Study of Acids, Bases and Salts (pH, indicators, preparation of salts); 4 Analytical Chemistry (action of NH4OH and NaOH on salt solutions, Fe/Cu/Zn/Pb/Ca/Al); 5 Mole Concept & Stoichiometry (Gay-Lussac, Avogadro, empirical/molecular formula, simple calculations); 6 Electrolysis; 7 Metallurgy (aluminium - Bayer/Hall-Heroult; steel/alloys); 8 Study of Compounds (HCl, Ammonia, Nitric acid, Sulphuric acid); 9 Organic Chemistry (alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, nomenclature, isomerism).
- Chapters 'Study of Compounds' are split into 4 sub-chapters in this repo (HCl, Ammonia, Nitric acid, Sulphuric acid).
- Deleted/added vs older syllabi: UNVERIFIED. Known history: 2016-2018 papers asked "Water" / "Hydrogen chloride" / "Fuels/ coal-petroleum" in other forms and "Practical Chemistry"; older Metallurgy included extra metals/extra uses; "Organic: IUPAC rules"; verify each old PYQ against the 2027 PDF.

## 6. Gotchas
- Equations must be balanced with conditions; store as plain text with subscripts (Unicode or HTML <sub>) and arrows. Provide state symbols only if board does.
- Structural/electron-dot diagrams need SVG/ASCII; limit "draw" tasks to MCQ-identify or text-describe.
- Salt analysis colours/observations must follow textbook (e.g. Cu(OH)2 pale blue, Fe(OH)3 reddish brown, Zn(OH)2 white soluble in excess NaOH, Pb(OH)2 white).
- Mole calculations: use given atomic masses; STP molar volume 22.4 dm^3.
- Q2(iii)/(iv) are objective-style: make "choose from bracket" and match tables.
