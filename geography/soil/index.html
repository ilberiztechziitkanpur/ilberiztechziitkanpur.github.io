Category & Origin Ex-situ (Transported) — Deposited by rivers (silt, sand, clay). In-situ (Residual) — Weathering of solidified lava / basalt rocks (Deccan Trap). In-situ (Residual) — Weathering of ancient crystalline & metamorphic rocks. In-situ (Residual) — Intense leaching due to heavy rain ($>200\text{ cm}$) & alternating wet/dry periods.
Colour & Reason Light grey to deep black/yellow (depends on depth & organic matter). Deep black to chestnut brown (iron & titanium compounds). Red due to high Iron Oxide ($Fe_2O_3$); looks yellow when hydrated. Reddish-brown (high iron oxide & aluminium left after leaching).
Texture & Key Property Coarse in upper valley; fine silt in delta. Bhangar (old, kankar) vs Khadar (new, fertile). Fine, clayey ($>60\%$ clay), high moisture-retentiveness, self-ploughing (cracks when dry). Porous, friable (loose), coarse, does not retain moisture; responds well to fertilizers + irrigation. Coarse, porous, acidic, infertile. Soft when wet, hardens like iron on exposure to air (used for bricks).
Rich In (Minerals) Potash & Lime (Ganga delta is also rich in Humus). L-I-M-P-C-A: Lime, Iron, Magnesium, Potash, Calcium, Alumina. Iron Oxide (and Potash in some areas). Iron Oxide & Aluminium compounds (Bauxite).
Deficient In Nitrogen, Phosphorus & Humus (except Ganga delta). Nitrogen, Phosphorus & Organic Matter (Humus). Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Lime, Magnesia & Humus. Lime, Silica (leached away), Nitrogen, Potash & Humus.
