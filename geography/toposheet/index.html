All Mathematical Equations & Working Formats (CISCE Marking Scheme)

In ICSE Geography, writing the final number without the unit (`km`, `km²`, `metres`, `hours/minutes`) loses `0.5` to `1` full mark. Memorize these exact equations and step-by-step layouts.

1. Area of Enclosed Grid Region (Full Grid Squares)

Used when Eastings ($E_1$ to $E_2$) and Northings ($N_1$ to $N_2$) are specified (e.g., 2027 Specimen Q1 iii-b):

Scale: 2 cm = 1 km ⇒ 1 Grid Square = 1 km × 1 km = 1 km²
Width (East-West) = (E₂ - E₁) × 1 km
Length (North-South) = (N₂ - N₁) × 1 km
Area = (E₂ - E₁) × (N₂ - N₁) sq. km (or km²)

Warning: If the question asks for the area "South of 94 Northing" on a standard 10×10 extract (like 2024 Board), multiply the full Easting width (`10` grid squares) by the number of grid squares south of 94 (`6` squares) = `60 km²`.

2. Direct (Crow-Fly) & Indirect Ground Distance

Standard Survey of India Map Scale is 1:50,000, which means 2 cm on map = 1 km on ground:

Map Distance = X cm (measured with ruler or twine)
Scale: 2 cm = 1 km
Actual Ground Distance = (X ÷ 2) km

Highway Distance Stone Trick (2027 Specimen Q1 ii-b): If asked for the length of the National Highway between Distance Stone `222` and Distance Stone `226`, each stone is `1 km` apart: 226 - 222 = 4 km (or measure with twine along the road and divide cm by 2).

3. Time, Speed & Distance Equation

Tested in 2024 Specimen ("Mohan cycles from 02 Northing to 12 Northing at 10 km/hr"):

Step 1: Ground Distance (D) = (N₂ - N₁) × 1 km
Step 2: Time (T) = Distance (D) ÷ Speed (S)
Example: D = (12 - 02) = 10 km; S = 10 km/hr
Time = 10 km ÷ 10 km/hr = 1 hour (or 60 minutes)

4. Six-Figure Grid Reference Equation

Pinpoints an exact point feature (Temple, Spot Height, Triangulated Height, Surveyed Tree):

Format: [E₁][E₂][e₃] [N₁][N₂][n₃] (Total 6 digits, NO spaces/hyphens)
• E₁E₂ = 2-digit Easting line to the LEFT of the object
• e₃ = Tenths (1 to 9) measured rightward inside the grid
• N₁N₂ = 2-digit Northing line BELOW the object
• n₃ = Tenths (1 to 9) measured upward inside the grid

CISCE Tolerance Rule: The Board Marking Scheme allows ±1 tolerance on the 3rd and 6th digits (e.g., if exact is `376966`, `375966` is also marked correct), provided the 4-figure base (`3796`) is 100% accurate!

5. Difference in Altitude / Elevation

Tested in 2026 Official Board Paper (Q1 v-a) & 2019 Series:

Highest Point on Map = Max Spot Height (.H) or ΔH (in metres)
Given Contour Height = C (in metres)
Difference in Altitude = (H - C) metres

Always scan the hilly forested corners (usually SE or NE on `G43S10` and `G43S7`) to locate the highest spot height or triangulated station on your sheet extract.

6. Relative Height / Depth (e.g., 12r, 6r, 3r)

Black `r` vs. Blue `r` is a classic examiner trap:

• Black "15r" on Sand Dune / River Bank / Embankment:
"Relative height of the sand dune / river bank / tank embankment is 15 metres."
• Blue "20r" next to a Lined Perennial Well:
"Relative depth of the lined perennial well is 20 metres."
