info Why are my Telegram uploads downloading as files?

Telegram only streams videos if they are strictly in MP4 format, encoded with the H.264 video codec and AAC audio codec. If you download an MKV or WebM from the web and upload it, Telegram treats it as a document file. By keeping "Optimize for Telegram" checked above, this tool will force the video into the exact format Telegram requires for instant streaming.
