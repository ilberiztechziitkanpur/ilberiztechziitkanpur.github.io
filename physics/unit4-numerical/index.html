⚡ 1. Current, Resistance & Networks
  • • Charge & Current: Q = n × e = I × t (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)
  • • Potential Diff. & Ohm's Law: V = W / Q = I × R
  • • Wire Resistance: R = ρ × (l / A) = ρ × l / (πr²)
  • • Uniform Stretching (V const.): R' = n²R, R₁ / R₂ = (l₁ / l₂)² = (r₂ / r₁)⁴
  • • Series Combination: Rs = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ (Current I same)
  • • Parallel Combination: 1/Rp = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ or Rp = (R₁ × R₂) / (R₁ + R₂)
  • • Branch Current Split: I₁ = I × [R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)], I₂ = I × [R₁ / (R₁ + R₂)]
🔋 2. Cell (ε, r), Power & Household Billing
  • • Cell Current: I = ε / (R + r)
  • • Terminal Voltage: V = I × R = ε − I × r
  • • Internal Drop: v = I × r = ε − V, and r = ((ε / V) − 1) × R
  • • Electrical Energy/Heat: W = H = VIt = I²Rt = (V² / R) × t
  • • Electrical Power: P = W / t = VI = I²R = V² / R
  • • Appliance Rating (P, V): Safe I = P / V, Filament R = V² / P
  • • Commercial Energy: E (in kWh) = [P (in W) × t (in hours)] / 1000
  • • Bill Cost: Cost = E (in kWh) × Rate per unit
🧲 3. Transformer & Mandatory Conversions
  • • Transformer Ratio: Es / Ep = Ns / Np = n (Turns Ratio)
  • • Ideal Transformer (Pout = Pin): Es × Is = Ep × Ip ⇒ Es / Ep = Ns / Np = Ip / Is
  • • Lorentz Force: F = B × I × l × sin θ (Max = BIl at 90°)
  • • 1 kWh to Joules: 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • • Length & Area: 1 mm = 10⁻³ m, 1 mm² = 10⁻⁶ m², 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m²
  • • Current & Power: 1 mA = 10⁻³ A, 1 μA = 10⁻⁶ A, 1 kW = 1000 W
  • • Time (for Joules): 1 min = 60 s, 1 hour = 3600 s
