[Specimen 2027 Q1(x)] A radioactive nucleus has 90 protons and 140 neutrons. It undergoes two successive alpha decays. What are the mass number and atomic number of the resulting daughter nucleus? (Ans: Mass number = 222, Atomic number = 86).
[ICSE 2026 Board Q1(xiv)] A lithium nucleus has 3 protons and 4 neutrons. What is its mass number? (Ans: 7).
✅ Official Answer: (a) In Y: p = 93, n = 144 ; In Z: total nucleons remain 237 (p = 94, n = 143)
Step-by-Step Calculation:
  • Step 1 (α-decay): An α-particle (42He) removes 2 protons and 2 neutrons. So in Y: Protons = 95 − 2 = 93, Neutrons = 146 − 2 = 144. Total nucleons (A) = 93 + 144 = 237.
  • Step 2 (β-decay): A neutron converts into a proton (10n → 11p + 0−1e). Thus Protons = 93 + 1 = 94, Neutrons = 144 − 1 = 143, while total nucleons (A = p + n) remain unchanged at 237!

Q6 • Master MCQ Very Hard • Competency 🔥 Repeated 4 Times 🎯 90% Probability
Asked In: CISCE Competency-Focused Practice Questions (CFPQ Q16) • ICSE 2018 • Specimen 2025
[CISCE CFPQ Item Bank Q16] Examine the following four nuclear equations:
I. 23592U + 10n → 14456Ba + 8936Kr + 3 10n
II. 2411Na → 2412Mg + 0−1e
III. 23892U → 23491Th + 42He
IV. 147N + 42He → 178O + 11H
Which of the above nuclear equations are correctly balanced?
🔒 Answer unlocks on DPP Submit
All Past Exam Variations of This Concept:
[CISCE CFPQ Item Bank MCQ Q16] Which of the given nuclear equations are correctly balanced for nucleon number and atomic number?
[ICSE 2018 Q10(b)(iii)] Write a balanced nuclear reaction when Uranium-238 emits an alpha particle to form a Thorium (Th) nucleus.
[ICSE 2026 Board Q9(iii)(b)] Write the balanced equation for the decay of 2411Na to 2412Mg.
✅ Official Answer: (c) I, II, and IV only
Explanation: In any nuclear reaction, both Mass Number (total A) and Atomic Number (total Z) must be conserved:
  • I is balanced: Mass number A: 235 + 1 = 144 + 89 + 3(1) = 236 ; Atomic number Z: 92 + 0 = 56 + 36 + 0 = 92.
  • II is balanced: A: 24 = 24 + 0 ; Z: 11 = 12 + (−1).
  • III is INCORRECT: Atomic number on the right-hand side is 91 + 2 = 93 ≠ 92 (Thorium has Z = 90, not 91).
  • IV is balanced: A: 14 + 4 = 17 + 1 = 18 ; Z: 7 + 2 = 8 + 1 = 9.

✅ Official CISCE Marking Scheme:
  • (i) Identification:
    • A = Alpha (α) radiation (positively charged, deflected slightly towards negative plate P2).
    • B = Gamma (γ) radiation (uncharged, passes undeviated).
    • C = Beta (β) radiation (negatively charged, deflected sharply towards positive plate P1).
  • (ii) Why Thick Lead Container with Narrow Opening: Radiations are emitted randomly in all directions. The thick lead walls absorb radiations going in unwanted directions (protecting surroundings) and allow only a fine, collimated beam to emerge through the narrow opening.
  • (iii) Why B is Unaffected & Max Speed: B (γ-rays) are electromagnetic waves carrying no electric charge, so they are not deflected by electric or magnetic fields. γ-radiation (B) travels with the maximum speed (3 × 108 m/s in vacuum).
  • (iv) Why C (β) Deflects More Than A (α): A β-particle (electron) has a negligibly small mass (≈ 1/7300th the mass of an α-particle), so its charge-to-mass ratio (q/m) is much larger and its inertia is much lower than an α-particle.
  • (v) Ascending Orders:
    • Ionizing Power: γ (B) < β (C) < α (A)
    • Penetrating Power: α (A) < β (C) < γ (B)
    • Biological Effect/Damage: α (A) < β (C) < γ (B) (for external exposure, since α is stopped by skin while γ penetrates deep organs).
  • (vi) Magnetic Field Rule: Fleming's Left-Hand Rule (remembering that the direction of conventional current is along the motion of α-particles and opposite to the motion of β-particles).
