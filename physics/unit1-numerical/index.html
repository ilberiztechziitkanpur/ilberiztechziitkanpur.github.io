1. Force, Torque & Principle of Moments
  • • Moment of Force: τ = F × d⊥ = F × r sin θ
  • • Moment of Couple: τ = F × d (d = couple arm / diameter)
  • • Sign Convention: Anticlockwise = +ve ; Clockwise = −ve
  • • Resultant Moment: τnet = τACW − τCW
  • • Principle of Moments: ΣτACW = ΣτCW ⇒ W1l1 = W2l2
  • • Uniform Metre Scale CG: Acts at 50 cm mark (Half-metre scale: 25 cm mark)
  • • Unit Conversions:
      1 N = 105 dyne ; 1 N m = 107 dyne cm
      1 kgf = g N (≈ 10 N) ; 1 kgf m = 9.8 N m (or 10 N m)
2. Work, Power, KE, PE & Conservation
  • • Work Done: W = F S cos θ = Area under F–x graph
  • • Incline Height: h = S sin θ ⇒ U = mg(S sin θ)
  • • Power: P = W / t = mgh / t = F × v
  • • Kinetic Energy: K = ½ mv2 = p2 / (2m) ⇒ p = √(2mK)
  • • Work-Energy Theorem: Wnet = F × S = ½ mv2 − ½ mu2
  • • Rebound / Friction Loss: ΔEloss = mg(h1 − h2)
  • • Unit Conversions:
      1 J = 107 erg ; 1 kWh = 3.6 × 106 J
      1 hp = 746 W = 0.746 kW ; 1 eV = 1.6 × 10−19 J
      g → kg (÷ 1000) ; km h−1 → m s−1 (× 5/18)
3. Machines: Levers & Pulley Systems
  • • Mechanical Advantage: MA = Load (L) / Effort (E)
  • • Velocity Ratio: VR = dE / dL = vE / vL
  • • Efficiency Relation: η = Work Out / Work In = MA / VR
  • • Ideal Lever: L × Load Arm = E × Effort Arm ⇒ MA = dE / dL
  • • Single Fixed Pulley: VR = 1, Ideal MA = 1 (dE = dL)
  • • Single Movable Pulley: VR = 2, Ideal MA = 2 (dE = 2dL)
  • • Block & Tackle (n pulleys):
      Downward E: VR = n ; Upward E: VR = n + 1
      With lower block weight w: L + w = nE ⇒ MA = n − (w/E)
